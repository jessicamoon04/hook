    What to do with a suspicious link

    Before you click

    • Find the real domain: the name right before .com, .net or similar. Everything to its left can be faked.
    • Be wary of urgency: “account locked”, “payment failed”, “verify within 24 hours”.
    • Go to the site yourself by typing the address or using its app, instead of following the link.

    If you already clicked

    • Close the tab without typing anything into the page.
    • Delete any file it downloaded, and run your antivirus scan.
    • Report the message as phishing in your email app so others are warned.

    If you entered a password

    • Change that password now, and on every other site where you reused it.
    • Turn on two-step verification for the account.
    • If you entered card or bank details, call your bank using the number on your card.
