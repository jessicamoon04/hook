What Hook Check does

Hook Check looks at the address of a link and points out common signs of phishing, such as fake brand names, misspellings, unusual endings and recently registered domains. It does not open the website or inspect what is on it.

Results are guidance, not a guarantee

The checks are automatic and can be wrong in both directions:

  • A link marked low risk can still be dangerous, for example a real website that has been hacked or a malicious file on an ordinary-looking site.
  • A link marked risky can turn out to be safe.

Use the result as one piece of information alongside your own judgement. Hook Check is not a replacement for security software, your bank’s advice or professional help. If you think you have been scammed, contact your bank or the relevant service directly.
