Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version:

  • Messages and links you paste are checked in your browser. We never receive them.
  • To check a website’s age, only its bare domain name (for example example.com) is looked up.
  • We don’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or accounts.

Who we are

Hook Check is run by Big Chi. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email jotesbychicollections@gmail.com.

What you paste into the checker

When you paste a link, email or text message, the checking happens inside your own browser. The text is not sent to us, not stored, and is gone when you close or reload the page.

Website age lookups

To tell you how old a website is, Hook Check sends only the domain name (for example example.com, never the full link or your message) to our server function. That function asks rdap.org (opens in a new tab), a public registration lookup service, which forwards the request to the registry for that domain ending.

We don’t keep a record of these lookups. To keep the site fast, our hosting provider may cache each answer (the domain name and its registration date) for up to 24 hours. The cached answer does not include who asked.

Your browser only ever talks to our own site. rdap.org and the registries see a request from our server, not from you, so they don’t receive your IP address.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel (opens in a new tab). Like any web host, Vercel automatically processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page address you requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Vercel’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

A shared result link contains the checked link in its address (after ?check=). When someone opens a shared result link, that address reaches Vercel like any other page request.

Fonts and other files

Every font, image and script on Hook Check is served from our own site. We don’t load anything from other companies’ servers, and we don’t embed videos, maps, social media widgets or other outside content.

Second-opinion buttons and outside links

The Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal buttons send the link you checked to that service, and only when you click them. Those services, and any other site we link to, have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and tracking

Hook Check does not set cookies, does not store anything on your device, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. See our Cookie Policy for details. The share buttons copy text to your clipboard, or open your device’s share menu. Nothing is sent to us.

Why we can handle this information

The only information involved is what is technically necessary to show you the site and run the check you asked for. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and the UK or EU GDPR where they apply, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing a working, secure service. We don’t sell information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone beyond the providers named on this page.

Children

Hook Check does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we don’t collect or store personal information ourselves, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Our hosting provider processes the technical data described above under its own policy. If you have a question or request, email jotesbychicollections@gmail.com and we’ll answer within 30 days. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (opens in a new tab) or the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Hook Check handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
