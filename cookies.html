Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version: Hook Check does not use cookies, and it does not store anything on your device. That’s why you don’t see a cookie banner.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Sites use them, and similar tools such as local storage, to remember you, keep you signed in, measure visits or show ads.

Links and messages you check stay in the page while it’s open and disappear when you close or reload it.

Our hosting provider

Hook Check is hosted by Vercel (opens in a new tab). If Vercel’s automatic protection detects suspicious traffic, it may ask your browser to pass a short security check and set a cookie that is strictly necessary for that check. Strictly necessary security cookies don’t need consent. See Vercel’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Other websites

Buttons such as “Check on Google Safe Browsing” take you to other websites, which have their own cookie policies.

Changes

If we ever start using cookies or similar storage, we’ll update this page first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
